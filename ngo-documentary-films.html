What I make

01

Impact and programme documentaries

Short films of 3 to 15 minutes that show what a programme changes in people's lives, for annual reports, websites, events and funders.

02

Beneficiary and field stories

Personal stories told in people's own words, filmed with consent and care, in Hindi or English with subtitles.

03

Donor, grant and CSR reports on film

Short films that turn a year of work or a grant cycle into something a funder will watch to the end.

04

Event and campaign films

Conferences, festivals, launches and campaigns, delivered as a highlight film plus vertical clips.

05

Reels and Shorts for your cause

Vertical cut-downs from every film for Instagram, LinkedIn and YouTube Shorts, so one shoot keeps working for months.

How it works

  1. A short call about the programme, the audience and where the film will be shown.
  2. A story plan: who we film, what we ask, and which moments show the change.
  3. The shoot, in the field or at your events, or I start from footage your team already has.
  4. Edit, colour grade, sound mix and subtitles, with a review round with your team.
  5. Delivery as a main film plus short versions for social, presentations and your website.

Questions

Who is this for?

NGOs, foundations, CSR teams, government programmes and social enterprises that need to show their work to donors, partners, funders and the public.

Do you only edit, or shoot as well?

Both. I can edit footage your team has already shot, or plan and film the story on the ground across India, including rural locations, and then edit, grade and mix it.

Can you work in Hindi?

Yes. Hindi is my first language, so I can interview people, follow conversations and cut Hindi and Hinglish footage without waiting for translations. I deliver English subtitles.

How do you handle consent and dignity?

People are filmed with informed consent, and I avoid images that reduce anyone to a symbol of need. I follow your organisation's safeguarding and child-protection policies.

What does it cost?

It depends on locations, shoot days and the number of films. I can work to grant and programme budgets. Send a few details and I'll reply with a quote.
