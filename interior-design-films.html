What I make

01

Home and project tours

A walk through a finished space, shot to show light, materials and detail. One horizontal film plus vertical cut-downs.

02

Product and collection stories

Furniture, textiles, lighting and objects filmed in use, so buyers see scale, texture and how a piece lives in a room.

03

Founder and sourcing stories

The people behind the brand: where pieces come from, how they are made, and why. The content that builds trust.

04

Store and studio films

Your showroom or studio as a place worth visiting, for your website, Google profile and social channels.

05

Monthly reel batches

A regular set of reels shot in one or two days and released through the month, captioned and ready to post.

How it works

  1. A short call about the space, the brand and where the films will be posted.
  2. A shot list built around the rooms, products and people that matter most.
  3. The shoot: usually a half or full day per location, with cinema camera and lighting.
  4. Edit, colour grade, captions and on-screen text, with one round of changes.
  5. Delivery in vertical and horizontal formats, sized for Instagram, YouTube and your website.

Questions

Who is this for?

Interior designers, architects, decor and furniture brands, home stores and design studios who want their spaces and products to look as good on screen as they do in person.

Do you travel for shoots?

I'm based in Unnao, between Lucknow and Kanpur, and shoot across India, including Delhi NCR. I have also filmed for a design studio in the United States, and I edit remotely for clients anywhere.

What do I get from one shoot?

Usually a set of vertical reels for Instagram and YouTube Shorts plus a longer horizontal film, all from the same shoot. We plan the list together before the shoot day.

Do you edit and colour grade too?

Yes. Every project is edited and colour graded in-house in DaVinci Resolve, with captions and on-screen text ready to post.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the number of locations, shoot days and deliverables. Send a few details through the contact form or WhatsApp and I'll reply with a quote.
